# Refund Policy

Employees and customers can request a refund for eligible purchases within 30 days of the original purchase date.

Refund requests must include the order number and reason for the request.

Approved refunds are normally processed within 5 business days.

Enterprise customers may have different refund terms defined in their individual contracts.

Digital products that have already been fully consumed may not qualify for a refund.

The finance team is responsible for approving refund exceptions.
