## Important Production Lessons

- Mock authentication is not production authentication.
- Client-provided tenant IDs cannot establish authorization.
- In-memory quota services are not distributed billing systems.
- Retrieval must enforce tenant and document permissions.
- LLM outputs need evaluation and safety controls.
- Production readiness requires real integrations and verified evidence.
