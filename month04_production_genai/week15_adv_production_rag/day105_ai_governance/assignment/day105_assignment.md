# Day 105 Assignment

## Task 1 — AI Registry
Register at least 3 AI systems with owner, purpose, model, risk, data type, environment and status.

## Task 2 — Model Registry
Register 3 models and track provider, model, version, approval status and purpose.

## Task 3 — Risk Register
Create at least 10 risks covering DATA_PRIVACY, SECURITY, HALLUCINATION, AUTHORIZATION, DATA_LEAKAGE, RAG_POISONING, TOOL_ABUSE, AVAILABILITY, COST and COMPLIANCE.

## Task 4 — Risk Calculation
For every risk calculate Impact × Likelihood.

## Task 5 — Data Governance
Create and test policies for PUBLIC, INTERNAL, CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED.

## Task 6 — Approval Workflow
Classify at least 5 actions as AI_ALLOWED, HUMAN_REQUIRED or DENIED.

## Task 7 — Model Change
Simulate v1 → v2 and require evaluation, security testing and approval before production approval.

## Task 8 — AI System Card
Complete the system card for one production-style AI system.

## Task 9 — Governance Review
Implement and test `needsReview()`.

## Task 10 — Evidence Package
Create sample risk, security, evaluation, approval and review evidence using synthetic/demo data only.
