# Retention Policy

The following are example learning-project retention periods:

| Data | Days |
|---|---:|
| Prompts | 30 |
| Responses | 30 |
| Security logs | 180 |
| Audit logs | 365 |
| Evaluation reports | 365 |
| Incident records | 365 |

These values are examples, not universal legal requirements. Actual retention should follow organizational policy and applicable requirements.
